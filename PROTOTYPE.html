OUR STORY

Founded in 2025, AromaGo bridge the gap between high-end perfumery and modern convenience. We believe that luxury shouldn't be gated behind department store counters.

Our network of proprietary "Smart Dispensers" maintain fragrances at the perfect molecular temperature, ensuring that every spray is as the perfumer intended.

Whether you need a quick refresh for an interview or a signature scent for a gala, AromaGo is your on-demand olfactory concierge.
